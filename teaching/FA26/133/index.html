Textbooks

Our primary text is the free, custom OpenStax Business Calculus edition. Daily reading assignments on the course schedule link directly to the relevant sections.

We will also draw from two free supplementary resources:

  • Mike May and Anneke Bart, Business Calculus with Excel, for business modeling, spreadsheet work, marginal analysis, and optimization.
  • OpenStax, College Algebra 2e, for selected algebra review and possible concluding topics.
Welcome to Math 133. Learning mathematics means doing mathematics. Plan to spend at least six hours each week outside class reviewing notes, discussing ideas, and working problems.
